# Wallet Manager Product Guide

## Initial Concept
Wallet Manager is a Flutter-based application designed to help store owners and businesses manage financial wallets, transactions, debts, and employee access.

## Target Audience
- **Freelancers/Vendors:** Individual vendors relying heavily on mobile wallets.

## Value Proposition
- **Unified Financial Tracking:** Centralizes tracking of multiple e-wallets, transactions, and debts to avoid manual math errors.
- **Automated Compliance:** Automatically enforces provider transaction limits and calculates commissions accurately.
- **Secure Employee Management:** Provides granular role-based permissions and audits for employee actions.

## Monetization Strategy
- **Subscription Model:** A recurring fee for continued access to premium features following an initial trial period.

## Future Growth
- **More Wallet Providers:** Integrating additional payment methods.
- **Multi-store Support:** Allowing owners to manage multiple physical store locations from one account.
